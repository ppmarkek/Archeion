# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Archeion is for people building a personal second brain for notes, study, and long-term knowledge work. They use it during focused reading and writing sessions, need context to remain visible, and expect their material to stay portable outside the application.

## Product Purpose

Archeion turns Markdown notes and learning files into a connected, navigable knowledge space. Success means a person can capture material quickly, understand how ideas relate, return to useful context, and keep the underlying `.md` files as the durable source of truth.

## Positioning

A calm, source-first second brain that combines portable Markdown with the spatial context of a modern knowledge workspace.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, and precise. The interface should feel like a quiet desk under directed light: dense enough to preserve context, restrained enough that reading and thinking remain central.

## Anti-references

Do not copy Notion or Obsidian branding, chrome, or layouts literally. Avoid noisy productivity dashboards, decorative effects without information value, saturated inactive states, and interfaces that hide the Markdown source or make the user's files feel proprietary.

## Design Principles

- Keep writing and reading at the visual center of every workspace.
- Reveal complexity progressively while keeping expert navigation close at hand.
- Treat Markdown files as durable user-owned sources, not opaque database records.
- Use spatial relationships to make knowledge easier to revisit and connect.
- Reserve the iris accent for focus, selection, and meaningful state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for interface and reading text, provide complete keyboard paths and visible focus, never rely on color alone to express graph or selection state, and respect reduced-motion preferences for every animated transition.
